# Tic Tac Toe

A Tic Tac Toe game for two players on one phone, built for Brick 1100 and drawn on the phone's
96 x 65 LCD pixels with `bridge.lcd`.

## How to play

- Take turns placing `X` and `O` in the grid. The first player to get 3 of their marks in a row wins.
- The box on the right shows whose turn it is.
- Use the number keys (`2`, `4`, `6`, `8`) to move the cursor, or the up and down keys to go through
  the cells in order.
- Press `OK` or `5` to place your mark.
- Press `0` to start over, or `C` to leave. Both ask first once a mark is on the board.
- When a game ends, press `OK` to play again, or `C` to leave.
